Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 3, 2026

The short version: DeskReset keeps your personal data to a minimum. Workout history and settings live on your device. Sign-in is handled by Google. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we are

DeskReset is a bodyweight exercise app for home office workers, developed by Noah Allard ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your workout history and session logs
  • App preferences and settings you configure
  • Your fitness level and program progress
  • Notification preferences and scheduled reminder times
  • Office hours and calendar preferences

Account & sign-in

DeskReset uses Google Sign-In, powered by Firebase Authentication, to create and manage your account. When you sign in with Google:

  • Google shares your name and email address with Firebase to create your account
  • Firebase stores a minimal account record (user ID, email, display name) to identify your session
  • We use your first name to personalise your in-app experience
  • We do not store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Google

Firebase Authentication is operated by Google LLC. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Calendar access

DeskReset may request access to your device calendar if you choose to schedule workouts. This access is:

  • Always optional — the app functions fully without it
  • Granted explicitly by you through your device's permission system
  • Used only to read availability and write workout events you request
  • Never used to read personal calendar content beyond scheduling needs

Calendar data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Payments & subscriptions

DeskReset offers an optional paid subscription. All payments are processed by the platform store you use (Google Play on Android, App Store on iOS). We do not collect or store your payment card details or any financial information.

Subscription status is verified through RevenueCat, a third-party service that communicates with the platform store on our behalf. RevenueCat receives your platform store customer ID and subscription status to confirm whether your subscription is active. No payment details are shared with RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Push notifications

DeskReset can send optional daily reminders to prompt you to take movement breaks. Notifications are:

  • Entirely optional — you control whether they are enabled and when they fire
  • Scheduled locally on your device using Android's alarm system
  • Not routed through any external notification server

Information we do not collect

DeskReset does not collect or use any of the following:

  • Location data
  • Health metrics or biometric data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reporting
  • Any data for advertising purposes

Children's privacy

DeskReset is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
