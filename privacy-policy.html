Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2026

The short version: DeskReset does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data beyond what is necessary to provide the features you use. Your calendar is accessed only with your explicit permission. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store — we never see your payment details. Sections marked Coming Soon describe features planned for a future version of the app.

Who we are

DeskReset is a bodyweight exercise app for home office workers, developed by Noah Allard ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we do not collect

DeskReset does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers beyond what is described in this policy. We do not collect:

  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reporting
  • Health or biometric data

Data stored on your device

DeskReset stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your workout history and session logs
  • App preferences and settings you configure
  • Your fitness level and program progress

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Calendar access

DeskReset may request access to your device calendar if you choose to schedule workouts. This access is:

  • Always optional — the app functions fully without it
  • Granted explicitly by you through your device's permission system
  • Used only to read availability and write workout events you request
  • Never used to read personal calendar content beyond scheduling needs

Calendar data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Payments & subscriptions

DeskReset offers optional paid features and subscriptions. All payments are processed by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS):

  • We never directly collect, see, or store your payment card details
  • The platform store shares your subscription status (active, expired, or cancelled) with the app solely to unlock the appropriate features
  • No financial data is transmitted to our servers

Account creation & sign-in

DeskReset offers optional account creation, including sign-in with Google or Apple. Account creation is never required to use the app's core features.

  • We request only the minimum information necessary — such as a display name or email address — to provide account functionality
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your account information with third parties
  • You may delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us

Push notifications

DeskReset offers optional push notifications to remind you to move and take breaks during your workday.

  • Notifications require your explicit permission through your device's system settings
  • You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings
  • Notification preferences are stored locally on your device
  • We do not use notifications to deliver advertising or share your data with third parties

Community & social features Coming Soon

A future version of DeskReset may include optional community or social features. If introduced, any information you choose to share in those features will be governed by an updated privacy policy provided at that time. Participation in any social features will always be optional.

Web version Coming Soon

A future web version of DeskReset may be made available. If introduced, a separate or updated privacy policy will be provided that addresses web-specific data practices such as cookies and browser storage.

Third-party services

DeskReset does not integrate with third-party analytics or advertising services. Payment processing is handled by Google Play and the Apple App Store as described above. If additional third-party services are introduced in the future, they will be disclosed in this policy before launch.

Children's privacy

DeskReset is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes — particularly those related to new data collection practices — we will provide notice within the app before the changes take effect.
